Student Finance Tracker

Every shilling tracked. Every decision informed.

Student Finance Tracker is a personal expense management tool built to solve a real problem — students losing track of where their money goes. Built entirely in vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no backend, no account, and no internet connection required after the first load. Your data stays on your device, private and fast.

Whether you are managing a tight monthly stipend, tracking textbook costs, or just trying to understand your spending habits, this tool gives you a clear picture. Set a monthly cap, get alerted when you are close to the limit, search your history with plain text or regex patterns, and export your records anytime as a JSON file.
